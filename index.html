    How to play

    Queen × Knight has three phases. Players alternate steps: a step is one placement, one capture, or one removal.

    1. Phase 1: placement. Place a Queen (2 points) or a Knight (1 point) on an empty square. A Queen may not see another Queen along a row, column or diagonal, and may not stand one knight-move from a Knight. A Knight may not attack a Queen. The first Knight needs no support; every later Knight needs another Knight one knight-move away. Once the board has at least 6 Queens and no more Queens than Knights, either player may start Phase 2 on their turn.
    2. Phase 2: captures. Click one of your pieces to select it as the attacker, then click a highlighted target. A Queen captures the first piece on each line. A Knight captures any piece one knight-move away. The captured piece is removed and the attacker moves onto its square. Points: Knight takes Queen 2, Queen takes Knight 1, Queen takes Queen 3, Knight takes Knight 0. If you may start Phase 3 and it is your turn, you can start it instead of capturing.
    3. Phase 3: removal. Click any piece to remove it: Queen 2 points, Knight 1 point. Removals alternate and the player who started Phase 3 removes first.

    The higher total score wins. Equal scores are a draw. If a phase cannot start because its count condition is not met and no further step is possible, the game is a draw.

    About the AI

    The AI plays the same rules as the game. In Phase 1 it places a Queen whenever a Queen square is legal, and a Knight only when none is left. In Phase 2 it searches ahead with alpha–beta pruning over all captures, and it chooses whether to start Phase 3 in the same search. In Phase 3 it removes Queens first.

    The rules engine and search are a JavaScript port of the Python simulator used in the project report, and the port is tested against the Python results. The game is a student project with custom rules, not a published game.
